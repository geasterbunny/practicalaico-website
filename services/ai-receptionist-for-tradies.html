THE CALL YOU DIDN'T TAKE

Most lost jobs never
show up as lost.

A missed call doesn't feel like a lost job. It feels like a number in your call log you'll get to at smoko. But someone with water through the ceiling, or a switchboard that keeps tripping, isn't waiting for smoko. If nobody answers, they try the next tradie, and by the time you call back the job's gone. You never hear about it, so it never feels like money.

Voicemail doesn't fix this. Plenty of callers hang up rather than leave a message, and the ones who do leave one still need you to stop work and ring back.

Count your own before you buy anything

Before you spend a dollar on this, find out whether you have the problem. It takes ten minutes:

  1. Open your phone's call log and go back two weeks.
  2. Count the missed calls from numbers that aren't in your contacts.
  3. Of those, count how many you didn't ring back within an hour.
  4. Multiply that by your average job value and by how often a callback lands the job.

If the answer is one or two calls a fortnight, a quick-callback habit is cheaper than any service, including ours. If it's one or two a day, keep reading.

Which one is right?

Low call volume: if you only miss a few calls a week, a message service or a disciplined callback habit is the cheaper answer. We'll tell you that if it's true.

Steady volume, mostly bookable work: quotes, service calls and maintenance jobs with predictable slots are where an AI receptionist earns its keep. It costs the same at fifty calls as at five, and it books the job rather than just taking a message.

Mostly emergencies: it can take the details and get them to you instantly, but you decide which emergencies it books and which ring through to you.

QUESTIONS TRADIES ASK

Straight answers.

What does an AI receptionist for tradies actually do?

It answers every call to your business number, day or night, in a natural voice. It gets the caller's name, number, address and what the job is, books it into a slot that fits the calendar rules you've set, and texts you a transcript and summary after every call. Anything outside your rules comes to you instead of being booked.

Can it book jobs into my calendar without me?

Only inside rules you approve up front, such as which days, which hours, how far you travel and which job types it may book. If a call falls outside those rules, it takes the details and the job comes to you to decide.
