QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

Straight answers.

How long does a website build take?

Most five-page sites are live within two weeks of you sending us your content. You see it on a private preview link before it goes live, and nothing is published until you approve it.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, the code is handed to you, and hosting is billed at cost. There's no platform fee and nothing that stops you taking it elsewhere.

My current site doesn't show up on Google. Can you fix it?

That's what Site Rescue is for. We crawl every page and link, check exactly what Google has indexed and what it has rejected, measure speed, and give you a plain-English report with a fixed price to fix it. If a rebuild is the honest answer, we'll say so.

What does "wired into your follow-ups" mean?

When someone fills in the enquiry form, a reply is drafted for you and held until you tap approve. Nothing is sent without you. It means an enquiry never sits unanswered because you were on a job.

Do you build Shopify or WooCommerce stores?

We build on Shopify for stores, and we can rescue an existing WooCommerce site. For a catalogue with more than a handful of products, ask about the Product Listing Engine as well.

Is there a sales call?

No. Email us what your business does and what you need the site to do. We reply within two business days with a fixed price. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.
