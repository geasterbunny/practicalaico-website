ChatGPT Work Mode vs Chat: Which to Use, and Why Work Hits Its Limit Sooner

There's a Chat and Work switch in ChatGPT now, and Work has its own usage limit that runs out faster than people expect. Here's when each mode is the right choice and how to stop hitting the wall halfway through a job.

Open ChatGPT this week and you'll probably see a switch between Chat and Work. ChatGPT work mode is OpenAI's name for the version that takes on a whole job, such as a report, a spreadsheet or a slide deck, and works through it step by step. It's useful. It also has its own usage limit, and that limit runs out much faster than Chat's.

If you've been told you're out of Work usage halfway through a task, or you're not sure which mode to pick, this is for you.

What each mode is for

OpenAI's help centre describes Chat as "fast, conversational assistance" and Work as an agent "designed for longer, multi-step work and finished deliverables".

Use Chat for: quick questions, rewording an email, brainstorming, summarising something you paste in, checking a formula. Anything where you want an answer in seconds and you'll do the rest.

Use Work for: a finished file. Research a topic and write it up, turn a folder of notes into a report, analyse a spreadsheet and build the charts, or create a presentation. Work can also run on a schedule, so it can produce the same report every Monday.

A simple test: if the result you want is a reply, use Chat. If the result you want is a file, use Work.

How to switch

On the web and in the mobile app, select Chat or Work from the dropdown. In the ChatGPT desktop app, choose ChatGPT from the menu, then pick Chat or Work from the toggle. Both kinds of chat appear together in your recent chats, and you can filter to show only one.

Projects work with both. Open a project and choose Chat for a normal conversation, or Work to start a task that uses the project's files and instructions.

Work is available on eligible paid plans. On company plans, your administrator decides whether you have it. OpenAI says Chat stays available whatever your workspace settings.

Why Work runs out sooner

Work doesn't draw on Chat's normal message limits. According to OpenAI, it follows the same usage structure as Codex, OpenAI's coding tool, and the two share the same limits and credits. On Plus and Pro, that allowance works in a rolling five-hour window plus a weekly limit.

A long Work task can use a lot of that allowance on its own, because it's doing many steps: searching, reading, writing, checking and rebuilding a file. OpenAI says the more powerful model options can use it faster, and longer tasks count for more.

File uploads, image generation and voice have their own separate limits, so running out of Work doesn't stop you using those.

How to check what you've got left

OpenAI says eligible users on personal plans and ChatGPT Business can see their remaining Work usage in the sidebar on the web, and can check reset times in Settings > Usage. When you hit a limit, the notice shows your options. Depending on your plan, that may include waiting until the reset time shown, adding credits, or upgrading. Plus and Pro users can buy credits without changing plans.

OpenAI Support can't reset your limits for you. On a company plan, your administrator may set a limit for each person, so ask them rather than support.

Make your Work allowance last

Plan in Chat, build in Work. Do the thinking, outlining and back-and-forth in Chat. Switch to Work only when you know exactly what you want built.

Give it everything up front. Most wasted Work usage comes from vague briefs that need two or three restarts. Attach the files, name the audience, and say what "done" looks like.

Create a two-page Word report for our operations manager from the three files attached. Audience: a busy manager who will read it on a phone. Structure: one-paragraph summary, three findings with one figure each, recommended next steps. Use only the attached files. If they disagree or a number is missing, list it at the end instead of guessing. Before you start, show me a short plan and wait for my OK.

Fix small things yourself. If one sentence or one cell is wrong, edit it by hand. Asking Work to redo it can trigger a whole new round of steps.

Pick a standard model unless you need more. The most capable options use your allowance faster. Use them for genuinely hard jobs, not for tidying a paragraph.

💡 Work makes the file, you still own it

A finished-looking document is easy to trust too quickly. Before you send anything Work produces, check every number against its source and read it as the person receiving it. Our guide to checking AI output before you send it has a five-minute routine.

Try it this week

Pick one job you'd normally spend an hour on that ends in a file, such as a monthly summary. Plan it in Chat first, then run it once in Work with the brief above. Afterwards, check your remaining usage in Settings > Usage, so you know how much a job like that costs you before you rely on it.
